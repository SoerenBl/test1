--- ENGLISH ---
Titel: Café Racer Motorcycles - Magazine
Beschreibung: A photographic editorial project on café racer builds — lean, radically stripped-down motorcycles that pair vintage character with a clean, sporty form language. The typography is idiosyncratic and unique, just like each individual build. All texts are set in the typeface I created myself: Gap Sans.
