--- ENGLISH ---
Titel: News
IntroText: Updates from the studio, projects and press.
Eintraege:
- 2026 | My project Legno Veloce is part of Targa Giovani 2026 - a category within the ADI Design Index, selecting 21 student projects from Italian universities out of a large number of submissions. ADI (Associazione per il Disegno Industriale) is Italy's industrial design association and administers the Compasso d'Oro, one of the oldest and most prestigious design awards. The exhibition was on view at the ADI Design Museum in Milan until 15 October, and continues as a touring exhibition to L'Aquila (Palazzo ex OMNI, 30 October to 17 November 2026).
